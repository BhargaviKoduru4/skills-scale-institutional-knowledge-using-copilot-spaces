# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project management lifecycle with clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and a focus on continuous learning. Projects begin with **initiation**, where the problem, desired outcomes, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, resources, and initial risks are identified before a go/no-go decision. During **planning**, the team turns the approved initiative into an actionable backlog, defines acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done, estimates work, maps milestones and releases, and records dependencies and risks.

During **execution**, the team tracks work on a project board, uses regular standups and delivery syncs to surface progress and blockers, and delivers small increments through pull requests, automated testing, reviews, and quality checks. Risks and blockers are escalated through the appropriate team, product, and sponsor channels. Before and during **release and deployment**, the team confirms acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, stakeholder communication, and rollback plans. After releases, milestones, or incidents, the team conducts **retrospectives**, records owned action items, and uses the results to drive continuous improvement.

Core roles include:

- **Project Managers:** coordinate schedules, delivery, risks, dependencies, and communication.
- **Product Managers:** define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure customer and business value.
- **Developers:** design, build, test, review, and document software.
- **QA/Testing:** validate quality, acceptance criteria, and critical user flows.
- **Stakeholders:** provide input, approvals, context, and feedback.
